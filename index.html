Questions

Answers Before
You Even Ask

How fast can you actually ramp up a new team?

Ramp timelines depend on the role, candidate availability, and training requirements. We provide estimated schedules during discovery and confirm timings in the proposal.

How do you handle data security and compliance?

We follow industry-standard security practices and define specific controls in each client engagement. Security, DPA terms, and access controls are agreed during onboarding.

What does pricing look like?

Pricing is scope-dependent. We provide transparent estimates based on role, hours, and service level. Volume discounts and managed-service pricing are available for longer engagements.

Do you handle non-English support?

Language coverage varies by role and region. We discuss language needs during discovery and propose staffing or tooling to meet your requirements.

What happens if we want to wind down?

Exit terms are defined in the contract. We aim for a clean handover of data and knowledge and provide transition support to minimize disruption.
